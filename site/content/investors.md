<section class="build-section container" id="the-problem" aria-labelledby="invest-problem-title">
<div class="build-section-heading"><h2 id="invest-problem-title">The problem</h2></div>
<div class="build-detail">

In a crisis, communications do not always disappear everywhere at once. One team may still have radio, another a mesh network, and a coordination point a satellite connection. The problem is that those systems often cannot exchange messages with each other.

That leaves information stuck where it was created, when the people who need it may be somewhere else.

</div>
</section>

<section class="build-section container" id="what-meshsat-does" aria-labelledby="invest-does-title">
<div class="build-section-heading"><h2 id="invest-does-title">What MeshSat does</h2></div>
<div class="build-detail">

MeshSat is an open-source routing layer that connects supported communications systems, including local radio, Meshtastic mesh, satellite, SMS and IP-based services.

A message can enter through one supported connection and leave through another, according to the configuration and routing rules chosen for the deployment.

The platform can run on a customer’s own hardware. MeshSat Hub is optional and can be hosted by the customer or used as a managed service.

</div>
</section>

<section class="build-section container" id="what-we-have-built" aria-labelledby="invest-built-title">
<div class="build-section-heading"><h2 id="invest-built-title">What we have built</h2></div>
<div class="build-detail">

MeshSat is at an early commercial stage, with working software, prototype hardware and demonstrated messaging paths.

- **Two V1 field-kit prototypes:** modular Raspberry Pi-based kits that connect selected radios, mesh and satellite equipment.
- **MSC-2:** a portable node designed to give a team access to mesh and satellite messaging.
- **MeshSat Hub:** a live service for managing devices and messages, also available for self-hosting.
- **Apps:** MeshSat [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.meshsat.android) and [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/meshsat/id6815748748) apps are available through their respective app stores.

We have demonstrated messages travelling over real satellite connections, including outdoor tests: a handheld message that reached a satellite and received a reply, and a phone-to-satellite round trip through the MSC-2.

The tests are recorded in the [MSC-2 test record](https://shop.meshsat.net/evidence/) and on [docs.meshsat.net](https://docs.meshsat.net/node/).

MeshSat has also received support from [SIDN fonds](https://www.sidnfonds.nl/projecten/meshsat-keeping-people-connected-when-the-network-is-not) for work on practical emergency communications.

The hardware remains at prototype stage and has not yet been used in a real emergency.

<section class="build-section container" id="who-it-is-for" aria-labelledby="invest-for-title">
<div class="build-section-heading"><h2 id="invest-for-title">Who it is for</h2></div>
<div class="build-detail">

MeshSat is designed for civilian organisations that need communications across different systems:

- Municipalities and emergency-support points.
- Civilian and volunteer search-and-rescue teams.
- Fire, medical and civil-protection organisations.
- Humanitarian organisations and NGOs.
- Critical-infrastructure operators.
- Mission-compatible communications integrators.
- Remote communities and outdoor teams.

A portable node, a field kit and a multi-site installation solve different problems. We intend to configure and price each deployment around the customer’s needs.

</div>
</section>

<section class="build-section container" id="how-we-reach-customers" aria-labelledby="invest-reach-title">
<div class="build-section-heading"><h2 id="invest-reach-title">How we reach customers</h2></div>
<div class="build-detail">

We are approaching civilian communications integrators and the organisations responsible for emergency preparedness: municipalities, safety regions, NGOs and emergency services. [SAFER/TOGETHER](https://safertogether.eu/) is our campaign for emergency support points, where MeshSat can help people exchange messages when ordinary networks are unavailable.

</div>
</section>

<section class="build-section container" id="how-meshsat-earns-money" aria-labelledby="invest-money-title">
<div class="build-section-heading"><h2 id="invest-money-title">How MeshSat earns money</h2></div>
<div class="build-detail">

The software is open source. The business is built around the products and services organisations need to put it to work:

- **Configured hardware:** portable nodes and field kits.
- **Tailored deployments:** equipment and configurations for individual teams or multiple sites.
- **Engineering and integration:** connecting supported systems and developing solutions with civilian partners.
- **Exercise days and training:** facilitated use of MeshSat during an organisation’s own exercise.
- **Managed services:** supported MeshSat Hub hosting and related services.

MeshSat is currently pre-revenue. The investment is intended to help the team develop these offers, reach customers and deliver the first paid projects.

</div>
</section>

<section class="build-section container" id="our-mission" aria-labelledby="invest-mission-title">
<div class="build-section-heading"><h2 id="invest-mission-title">Our mission is&nbsp;non-negotiable</h2></div>
<div class="build-detail">
<div class="build-prototype invest-mission">

**MeshSat is built for civilians, not warfare.**

We do not work with organisations that develop, produce, integrate or operate weapon systems, and we do not provide MeshSat for use in a weapon system. This applies to customers, partners and investment, with organisations assessed together with their group.

Our mission includes humanitarian relief, civilian search and rescue, disaster response, fire and medical services, municipalities and critical-infrastructure resilience.

<section class="build-section container" id="the-investment" aria-labelledby="invest-investment-title">
<div class="build-section-heading"><h2 id="invest-investment-title">What the investment will make possible</h2></div>
<div class="build-detail">

We are seeking **€300.000 in early-stage investment** to let the three founders work full-time on MeshSat and build the business around the platform.

The funding is intended to support:

- Engineering, product refinement and customer delivery.
- Sales, campaigns and mission-compatible partnerships.
- Equipment, operating costs and working capital.
- The formation and operation of a Dutch BV.

We are interested in an investor who can bring relevant experience, relationships or operational insight as well as capital, and who will uphold MeshSat’s civilian mission.

MeshSat currently operates through Elli.Z.G. The proposed BV has not yet been formed.

</div>
