05 / USE CASES FOR EVALUATION

Integration scenarios

These are research scenarios. No customer deployment, fleet compatibility or operational approval is claimed.

MSC-3TL FUTURE PLAN

TETRA teams and volunteer mesh

Evaluate selected text and position exchange between a professional radio team and Meshtastic volunteers, with Iridium for remote reporting. Routing policy controls what crosses the boundary.

MSC-3TD FUTURE PLAN

Teams using different radio systems

Evaluate message exchange between TETRA and DMR teams working in direct mode. Fleet-specific data formats, channel authorization and encryption handling must be established first.

MSC-3DL FUTURE PLAN

DMR teams and local sensors

Evaluate DMR text and supported location traffic alongside Meshtastic field reports. Test which message types can be translated and forwarded over the common satellite path.
