# Reticulum

Every workspace can have an LXMF address on the Hub's Reticulum node. People using Sideband,
CrossTalk, MeshChat, NomadNet or any other LXMF client message it; what arrives is in the inbox on
the **Reticulum** page (under Network), and what you send from there is marked delivered when the
other side proves it. An owner creates the address on that page.

![Reticulum page: the workspace's LXMF address with its QR code, where a client connects, who can reach the address (Private, with a network name and a passphrase), the propagation node and the address settings](/images/hub/reticulum-2026-10-27.webp)

## Your address

The address is a Reticulum identity the Hub keeps for your workspace, shown as a 32-character hash
with a QR code. Other clients reach it by that hash and show the display name you set once they
have heard the address announce. It is announced every 30 minutes and at once after a change.

A **stamp cost** makes a sender do proof of work for each message. 0 accepts every message, which is
Sideband's default; costs above 16 keep most clients out.

## Who can reach it

You choose, on the Reticulum page:

| Setting | Who reaches the address |
|---|---|
| **Private** (the default) | Only clients that enter your workspace's network name and passphrase |
| **Public** | Anyone on Reticulum, the way anyone can write to an email address |
| **Off** | Nobody; the address is not announced |

The network name and passphrase are generated by the Hub and shown on the page to owners and
operators. **Replace passphrase** makes new ones and disconnects at once every client that joined
with the old ones; everybody you still want in enters the new ones.

What Private covers, and what it does not:

- It covers what the Hub carries. A member's own app may still announce itself on other Reticulum
  networks it is connected to.
- Anyone who has the passphrase can pass it on. The only way to remove one person is to replace
  the passphrase for everyone.
- Private is a closed network: from it, your address does not reach public Reticulum destinations
  through the Hub.
- Your kits join your workspace's Private network by themselves. The Hub hands each kit the
  network name and passphrase, and the new ones within seconds when you replace them; nobody types
  them into a kit. The kit connects to `rns.meshsat.net` port 4242 the way an app does, and on that
  link it carries only its own messages: it never connects your workspace's network to its LoRa
  mesh or its other links. When the address is Public or Off, the kits leave. A kit you delete or
  lose keeps the passphrase it was given, so replace the passphrase. A kit can be kept out with
  `MESHSAT_HUB_RNS_JOIN=false`. A kit takes at most 60 messages a minute on its own address.

Switching from Public to Off stops the Hub serving the address, but a client that heard it while it
was Public keeps it in its own cache until that expires.

## Connect a client

All clients connect to the same place, `rns.meshsat.net` port 4242, with a TCP client interface.

**Sideband**: Connectivity, Connect via TCP. Enter `rns.meshsat.net` as the host and `4242` as the
port. For a Private address, also enter the network name and the passphrase from the Reticulum
page.

**MeshChat, NomadNet, `rnsd`** and anything else that reads a Reticulum config:

```ini
[[MeshSat Hub]]
  type = TCPClientInterface
  enabled = yes
  target_host = rns.meshsat.net
  target_port = 4242
  # For a Private address:
  network_name = <network name from the Reticulum page>
  passphrase = <passphrase from the Reticulum page>
```

Then paste the address into the client's new-conversation field, or scan the QR code. The address
appears in the client within a minute of connecting.

## The propagation node

Each address comes with a propagation node on the same identity, shown on the page. Set it as the
propagation node in a client and it holds messages for that client while it is offline, and hands
them over when the client syncs. Messages for your own address arrive in the inbox directly.

## What has been tested

Tested on 27 Oct 2026 with the Reticulum and LXMF releases Sideband ships (RNS 1.5.7, LXMF 1.2.1),
from outside the Hub through `rns.meshsat.net:4242`:

- With the network name and passphrase, a client got a path in under half a second and its message
  was delivered.
- Without them, or with another workspace's, a client got no path.
- Public admitted a client without a code; Off admitted nobody.
- After **Replace passphrase**, the old code got no path and a client connected with it was
  disconnected; the new code worked.

Not tested yet: a run in the Sideband app itself.
