# Your team

Crew and Fleet are team plans. Here is how somebody else gets in.

## Inviting

Open **Settings** and send an invite to their email address. They sign up the normal way and the
invite is matched to the verified address on their MeshSat ID the first time they sign in, which
is why the address has to be the one they will actually use.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Viewer | see everything: map, messages, devices, positions |
| Operator | that, plus send messages and manage devices |
| Owner | that, plus billing, invites, API keys, provider accounts and the audit log |

The person who created the account is its first owner. You can have more than one.

Pick the smallest role that lets somebody do their job. An operator can spend your airtime; a
viewer cannot.

## Removing somebody

Revoke an unaccepted invite or remove a user from **Settings**. Their access ends immediately,
and what they did stays in the audit log.
