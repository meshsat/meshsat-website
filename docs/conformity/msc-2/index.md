# MeshSat Compact v2 (MSC-2): EU conformity documents

This is the address printed on the label of every MeshSat Compact v2, model MSC-2, serial numbers MSC2-2026-0001 onward. The unit is a portable messaging gateway between Meshtastic LoRa (869,4 to 869,65 MHz), Bluetooth Low Energy and the Iridium satellite network, with a GNSS receiver. This page holds the documents the Radio Equipment Directive asks the manufacturer to make available: the EU declaration of conformity and the instructions and safety information that ship with the unit. Every edition ever issued stays here.

## Declaration of conformity

Hereby, Elli.Z.G., trading as MeshSat, declares that the radio equipment type MeshSat Compact v2 (MSC-2) is in compliance with Directive 2014/53/EU. The full text of the EU declaration of conformity is available at the following internet address: [https://docs.meshsat.net/conformity/msc-2/MeshSat-Compact-v2-MSC-2_EU-Declaration-of-Conformity.pdf](/conformity/msc-2/MeshSat-Compact-v2-MSC-2_EU-Declaration-of-Conformity.pdf)
