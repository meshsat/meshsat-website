A shared link for the team.

The idea: local mesh radios, one satellite gateway, one airtime line.

MSC-2 combines a LILYGO T-Beam Supreme and a RockBLOCK 9704-SMA in one compact enclosure. The goal: the team keeps its own LoRa radios and shares one satellite gateway and one airtime subscription, instead of carrying a satellite messenger and a plan for every person.

A phone with the MeshSat app for Android or iOS connects over Bluetooth and reaches both radios. Without a phone, the gateway is designed to forward selected messages from the mesh into the modem on its own. That path has been tested on the bench; the satellite leg from the complete unit is the next test.

Two radios, brought together.

  • LILYGO T-Beam Supreme

    The Meshtastic-compatible LoRa side of the design: the 868 MHz radio, the display, GPS and the Bluetooth link to a phone. Its three buttons sit on the side of the unit.

  • RockBLOCK 9704-SMA

    The Iridium IMT satellite side: the modem that carries messages to and from the Iridium network, on an airtime line rented from Ground Control. The IMT service carries messages up to 100 kB (Ground Control's figure); what each MSC-2 path supports is being measured.

  • One 18650 cell

    A planned 3.400 mAh Li-ion cell in the cradle between the two boards, charged over USB-C at 5 V and 1 A.

  • The orange ASA enclosure

    A 3D-printed case, approximately 146 x 63 x 63 mm for body and lid, with a display window, two antenna sockets, a sealed USB-C socket and the quick-message button. The button is intended to send a preset message to a preset contact. It is not a monitored distress button.

What you need separately

  • Satellite airtime. Purchased separately from Ground Control. An active IMT service is required, and access fees and usage charges depend on your plan. Check the current provider terms before ordering. One line serves the whole team.
  • Mesh radios. A compatible radio for each person who sends from the mesh, with matching radio and channel settings. The unit itself is one.
  • A phone, if you want one. The MeshSat app for Android or iOS, free and open source; the app documentation lists the supported platforms and what works with MSC-2 at launch.

Does the price include satellite service?

No. Ground Control IMT airtime is purchased separately. Check the provider's current plans for access fees and usage charges.

Will it work with my Meshtastic radio?

Your radio needs compatible hardware and firmware and matching radio and channel settings. The node documentation lists the devices tested so far; not every Meshtastic device has been tested.

Can it operate without a phone?

Phone-free forwarding from the mesh into the RockBLOCK 9704 has been tested on the bench, per the current MSC-2 test record. The first satellite pass from the complete unit is the next test.

Is the enclosure waterproof?

The design is sealed with an O-ring and sealed sockets; the assembled enclosure's water resistance is tested on the pilot unit, and no rating is claimed before that.

How long does the battery last?

Battery life is measured on the pilot unit; a cell's capacity alone does not establish runtime, so no figure is claimed before that.

Is it suitable as an emergency beacon?

No. MSC-2 is a prototype messaging gateway. It does not include a monitored emergency response or rescue service.
