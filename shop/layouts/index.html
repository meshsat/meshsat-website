Does the price include satellite service?+

No. The planned launch price covers the hardware package. Satellite airtime is purchased separately from Ground Control. Access fees and usage depend on the plan you choose.

Will it work with my Meshtastic radio?+

The design is for compatible radios on the configured mesh, using matching radio and channel settings. It does not automatically bridge every radio within range.

Can it operate without a phone?+

Routing selected mesh messages into the modem without a phone has been bench tested. The complete MSC-2 satellite workflow still needs its first satellite test.

Is it waterproof?+

The enclosure has an O-ring design, but no tested water-resistance rating is claimed. Testing is planned for the pilot unit.

How long does the battery last?+

Battery life has not been measured on the complete pilot unit. A figure will be published after testing.

Is it an emergency beacon?+

No. MSC-2 is a prototype and is not an emergency service or distress device. The red quick-message button is not monitored, and no rescue service is included.
