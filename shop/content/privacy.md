This notice covers shop.meshsat.net: the mailing list, orders once ordering opens, the withdrawal form, support e-mail and analytics. The MeshSat apps and the Bridge send us nothing; their notice is on [meshsat.net/privacy/](https://meshsat.net/privacy/).

## The mailing list

When you ask to be notified about pre-orders we store your e-mail address, your name if you give it, and the time you signed up and the time you confirmed. We send one mail to confirm the address; nothing is stored as confirmed until you click the link in it. Then we write about MeshSat Compact v2 pre-orders and launch news only. The list runs on listmonk, software we host on our own servers in the Netherlands; no third party sees it. Legal basis: your consent. We keep the address until you unsubscribe, which every mail lets you do with one click, or until you ask us at support@. Kickstarter's own notification list, if you follow the project there, is Kickstarter's and under Kickstarter's notice.

## Orders

When ordering is open and you buy a unit we store your name, delivery and billing address, e-mail address, telephone number for the carrier, what you ordered, what you paid and when, and the serial number of the unit you received. Payment is taken by Stripe, which sees your card or bank details; we never do. Stripe processes that data under its own notice as an independent controller for the payment. The order lives in our order system on hub.meshsat.net, on our own servers. The invoice and any credit note are issued by Invoice Ninja, software we host ourselves. Legal basis: the contract with you and, for invoices, the Dutch tax law that makes us keep them for seven years. The carrier receives your name, address and telephone number to deliver the parcel.

## The withdrawal form

The online withdrawal function stores your name, the order or invoice number, your e-mail address, the reason if you give one, and the date and time you sent it. We keep it with the order, for the same seven years, because it is part of the contract's record.

## Support e-mail

Mail to support@meshsat.net is kept as long as the matter is open and for the time the law requires afterwards. It stays on our own mail server.

## Analytics

We use Umami, self-hosted on our own infrastructure. It sets no cookies and builds no profile of you; it counts page views and which buttons were used. Nothing is shared with anyone.

## Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to its use, or receive it in a portable form, by writing to [support@meshsat.net](mailto:support@meshsat.net). We answer within a month. Invoices stay for the seven years the tax law requires even when you ask for deletion. You may complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

## Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anyone on the mailing list is told in the next mail.
