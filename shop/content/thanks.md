title: "Thank you"
description: "What happens after you order a MeshSat Compact v2."
---

Your order is placed. Within a few minutes you receive an e-mail from billing@meshsat.net with your receipt as a PDF; if it does not arrive, look in the spam folder and then write to [support@meshsat.net](mailto:support@meshsat.net).

## What happens next

We build and test your unit in Leiden and ship it in the window shown on the product page, by insured parcel, and send you the tracking number by e-mail. If you placed a pre-order with a deposit, the invoice for the rest reaches you when the unit is ready, and it ships when that invoice is paid.

## Changing your mind

You may withdraw from the purchase within 14 days of receiving the unit, without giving a reason: [right of withdrawal](/withdrawal/). A pre-order can be cancelled at any time before dispatch, and the deposit comes back within 14 days.
